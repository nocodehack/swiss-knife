The 3 fixes

“Claude waited 40 minutes for me to click Allow.”

A chime, a desktop notification and a red strip the moment Claude needs you, plus a second ping after 5 minutes.

Fix 3 of 3

“Long chats eat your tokens.”

Every message re-reads the whole chat. At ~70% full, one click writes a handoff note. Open a new chat and Claude picks up exactly where you left off. No /clear.

Everything else in the box

Each one can be turned on or off in Settings.

  • Time & cost per projectHow long you worked and what it’s worth. Breaks don’t count.
  • Daily budgetSet a daily amount per project. A heads-up at 80% and 100%.
  • GuardAsks you first before a command that could delete your work.
  • Last-task recapWhat got done, what’s still needed, and which files changed.
  • Model tipsSays when a cheaper or stronger model fits the job better.
  • Quick-memory timerWarns before a long chat’s quick memory runs out and the next message costs more.
  • PinsPin any reply. Plans you approve are pinned for you.
  • Hide secrets on screenPasswords, keys and emails show as a grey bar in the prompt box and the chat when you record or share your screen.
  • /report for clients1–3 plain lines on what you did today or this week, with time and cost.
  • Pixel mascotShows what Claude is doing, and naps on your break.

How to install, in 3 steps

  1. 1
    Update Claude Code.

    In a terminal: claude update

  2. 2
    Paste the line.
    /plugin install swiss-knife --marketplace nocodehack/swiss-knife
  3. 3
    Press y, then Enter.

Mods install through Claude Code’s plugin system, so the command says plugin.

In the desktop app: + → Plugins → Add marketplace → nocodehack/swiss-knife

Safe and private

  • Guard is a seatbelt, not a vault: it asks first and never approves anything by itself.
  • Your numbers stay on your computer.
  • The handoff, recap, /report and model tips send text to Claude with your own login. No API key.
